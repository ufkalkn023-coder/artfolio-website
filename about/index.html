ABOUT ARTFOLIO

Art, carefully
curated.

Artfolio is an independent visual arts publishing project focused on curating and presenting artworks, artists, movements, and stories from art history.

The project creates and distributes educational and editorial visual-art content across digital platforms. Where appropriate, Artfolio prioritizes public-domain artworks and materials that can be lawfully reproduced.

Artfolio Content Publisher

Artfolio Content Publisher is an internal content publishing application used solely by Artfolio to manage and publish prepared visual-art content.

The application may connect to third-party platforms, including Pinterest, through official APIs and OAuth authorization.

For Pinterest, the application allows the authorized Artfolio account owner to select prepared content, choose a Pinterest board, provide a Pin title, description and destination URL, and create or schedule Pins.

The application accesses only the data belonging to accounts explicitly authorized by the account owner.

Artfolio Content Publisher does not scrape Pinterest, automate follows, likes, comments, or other engagement, collect Pinterest passwords, sell Pinterest data, or provide Pinterest data to third parties.

The application is currently intended for internal, single-user use by Artfolio.
